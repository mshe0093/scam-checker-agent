# Code of conduct

Scam Checker exists to protect people, and many of them are not technical. Some have just lost money.
This code covers how we treat each other, and also how we treat the people in the messages we share.

It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code comments and any other space
connected to this project.

## Our standards

### 1. Be kind and assume good faith

- Disagree with ideas, never attack people.
- Explain technical points plainly. Many contributors know a lot about scams and little about code,
  and the reverse is true too. Both kinds of knowledge matter here.
- English and Bahasa Malaysia are both welcome, and so is a mix of the two. Nobody should be corrected
  for their grammar.

### 2. Never blame victims

Scams are designed by professionals to fool careful people. When you talk about a message, a
report or a loss, don't say or imply that the person was stupid, greedy or careless.
"How did anyone fall for this?" isn't allowed here.

### 3. Protect the privacy of the people in the messages

Real scam messages often contain real people's details. Before you post a message anywhere in this project:

- Remove or replace personal details: names, IC and passport numbers, account and card numbers,
  addresses, and the phone numbers of anyone who isn't the scammer. Use placeholders such as `[NAME]`.
- Never post a working TAC, OTP or PIN, even an expired one.
- Don't post screenshots of someone else's chat without their permission.
- Don't try to identify, contact or "expose" anyone named in a message, including suspected scammers.
  Report scams to the authorities (NSRC 997, PDRM, or your bank). Don't take matters into your own hands.

Maintainers will edit or delete posts that break this rule, without asking first.

### 4. Handle scam links safely

- Defang every malicious link you post, for example `hxxps://fake-bank[.]top/login`. The tools
  understand defanged links, and readers won't click them by accident.
- Don't post links to live phishing kits, malware or APK files except in defanged form.

### 5. Use the project to protect, not to harm

Don't use this project's spaces to:

- develop, test or improve scams, phishing kits or malware
- ask for or share ways to get scam messages past this checker or other filters, except through the
  private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
- collect or share other people's personal data

Ways to fool the checker are welcome **as private security reports**, because that's how it gets better.
Don't post them publicly before they're fixed.

### 6. Be honest about evidence

This project tries to say "couldn't verify" rather than guess. Apply the same standard in discussions:

- Cite your source for a hotline, domain or scam pattern.
- Say when you're unsure.
- Don't present an unconfirmed claim as fact.

## Unacceptable behaviour

As well as breaking the standards above, these are not allowed:

- harassment, threats or intimidation
- insults, slurs or demeaning jokes about anyone's race, religion, nationality, language, gender,
  sexuality, disability, age or background
- sexual language or imagery
- trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions
- publishing anyone's private information ("doxxing")
- promoting products, services or investment schemes

## Reporting

If you see behaviour that breaks this code, or someone has posted personal data:

- **Personal data or a live malicious link:** report it to the maintainer straight away, and we'll remove it first and talk afterwards.
- **Anything else:** contact the maintainer ([@mshe0093](https://github.com/mshe0093)) privately. You can also use GitHub's
  **Report content** option on any comment, issue or pull request.

Reports are kept confidential. Nobody will face retaliation for a report made in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what action to take, using these steps as a guide:

| Step | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | A one-off mistake, such as an unredacted message posted by accident | The content is edited or removed, with a private note explaining why |
| 2. Warning | A pattern of poor behaviour, or one harmful act | A written warning, and the person is asked to stop interacting with the people involved for a while |
| 3. Temporary ban | Serious or repeated breaches | No participation in the project for a set period |
| 4. Permanent ban | Harassment, doxxing, or using the project to help scams | Permanently removed from the project |

Steps can be skipped when the harm is serious. Posting personal data or helping scams can lead straight to step 4.
